How It Works

Three steps to immortalize your Bitcoin legacy

1

Create Your Will

Register your heirs, their inheritance percentages, and a proof-of-life period directly on Bitcoin L1.

2

Stay Alive, Ping

Periodically send a "ping" transaction to prove you're active. Every ping resets the inactivity clock.

3

Automatic Inheritance

If no ping arrives within your set period, heirs can claim their inheritance — without a court order.

4

Update Anytime

Life changes. Revoke or update your will at any time while you're active. You always stay in control.

🔐

100% Non-Custodial

Your tokens never leave your wallet until execution. The contract only holds approved transfer rights.

⚡

Bitcoin Native

Built with OP_NET on Bitcoin L1. No sidechains, no bridges, no wrapped assets. Pure Bitcoin.

⚖️

Multi-Heir Split

Up to 10 heirs with custom percentage splits. Assign different amounts to each beneficiary precisely.
